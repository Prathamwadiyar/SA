## The idea

What if a fragrance website communicated atmosphere before asking visitors to shop? **Aurél** explores how quiet typography, careful proportion, and warm, sculptural photography can make an everyday product feel considered.

## Creative direction

The fictional fragrance house is represented through a deep wine palette, restrained serif typography, and a product-first interface. The composition intentionally leaves room for mood and materiality instead of filling every inch with sales messages.

## Digital exploration

This is a **self-initiated visual concept**, not a commissioned client site or a published commercial store. The work explores campaign art direction and the hierarchy of a potential ecommerce experience.

## What this study demonstrates

A point of view on luxury digital storytelling: establish identity, create visual restraint, and give meaningful interactions enough room to breathe.
